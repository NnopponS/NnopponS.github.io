FIELD NOTE / 02

WheelAthlete

Before analyzing wheelchair motion, you have to trust the recording.

WheelAthlete synchronizes inertial sensors on the left and right wheelchair wheels. Its Windows application keeps collection separate from plotting and review, so a busy interface is not in charge of the raw data.

02 / WORKFLOW

From trial to trace.

  1. 01 / RECORD

    Synchronize the sensors

    The acquisition daemon handles BLE, clock synchronization and packet sequencing. A center IMU is optional for research instrumentation.

  2. 02 / CHECK

    Inspect what was captured

    The Windows interface exposes recording quality and diagnostics, including sequence loss, so dropped packets do not go unnoticed.

  3. 03 / EXPORT

    Keep the trail inspectable

    Experiments and trials stay organized for CSV export and offline analysis. The raw recording path remains separate from the interface.

03 / SCOPE

What it doesn’t claim.

This is research tooling for acquiring and reviewing motion data. It is separate from the WheelSense assistive-care project, and does not claim clinical validation or guaranteed Bluetooth performance across Windows setups.

Use the repository documentation to run from source and verify the supported hardware and software versions for your setup.
